Self-help by topic

😰 Anxiety & Stress

Grounding (5-4-3-2-1 technique), box breathing, and progressive muscle relaxation can help calm an anxious body and mind in the moment.

😔 Low mood & Depression

Small, consistent actions — a short walk, sunlight, reaching out to one person — can help even when motivation is low. Be patient with yourself.

😴 Sleep

A consistent sleep schedule, reduced screen time before bed, and a wind-down routine improve sleep quality over time.

🎓 Academic burnout

Break tasks into small chunks, use short focused study sprints with breaks (e.g. Pomodoro technique), and protect at least one rest day a week.

🤝 Loneliness

Joining a club, volunteering, or simply messaging one person you trust can be a small but powerful first step.

🧑‍⚕️ Professional help

Most colleges have a counseling cell — consider booking a session. Therapy is a sign of strength, not weakness.
